Professional services for courts and justice agencies

Professional services means hands-on expert help: people who plan, buy, launch and improve technology alongside your staff. Ours are independent and vendor-neutral, for courts and justice agencies, delivered as fixed-scope projects or ongoing hourly support. Most clients combine two or three services.

Planning and procurement

Before you buy or change anything.

  1. 1

    Current state assessment

    Find out why things aren’t working.

    We sit with your clerks, judges and staff and study how your systems, processes and data run today.

    • An audit of how your systems are configured
    • Findings ranked by priority
    • A roadmap you can act on
    Ask about an assessment
  2. 2

    Procurement support

    Buy the right system, on terms that hold.

    We run the procurement from first draft to signed contract, so you have an independent voice in the room.

    • Your needs, written from your operational and regulatory duties
    • The RFP, requirements and evaluation criteria
    • A review of vendor scope and statements of work before you sign
    Ask about procurement

Implementation services

While a project is underway.

  1. 3

    Project management

    Get a project moving and keep it moving.

    A PMP-certified principal plans and runs the work from kickoff to close.

    • Schedule and budget, tracked
    • Risks and vendor commitments in view
    • Clear status for leadership at every step
    Ask about project management
  2. 4

    Implementation and hypercare

    Go live with someone in your corner.

    We get your organization ready, then work beside your team through cutover. We hold the vendor to the requirements you agreed on.

    • Readiness planning
    • Hands-on help with configuration, testing and cutover
    • Support through stabilization after go-live
    Ask about implementation
  3. 5

    Integrations and data

    Get your data flowing and your reports matching.

    We design and review interfaces with justice partners, state agencies and peripheral systems, so no one has to rekey data.

    • Interface design and review
    • Data analysis that surfaces quality issues and trends
    • Reporting you can trust
    • A plan for moving your data when a system changes
    Ask about integrations

Ongoing services

Once a system is live.

  1. 6

    Support and optimization

    Clear out the workarounds.

    We keep your system working well after go-live, so you get full value from what you already own.

    • Issues resolved and configuration tuned
    • Simpler processes behind the system
    • New features set up, documented and taught to staff
    Ask about support
  2. 7

    Product management

    Build custom software that fits the work.

    We own the product on your side, so what gets built matches how your staff do the work.

    • A product roadmap, kept current

About us

Cairn Advisory is an independent professional services firm for courts and justice agencies. Our team has spent more than 15 years building and implementing court and justice software, from product management on the development side to statewide rollouts, and includes former court staff.

We have seen technology projects from the agency side and the vendor side, and we know where they tend to go off course. Because we answer to no vendor, our advice starts with how your people actually do the work.

Why “Cairn”?

On a mountain trail, a cairn is a small stack of stones left by a hiker who came before. It tells the next person the route is sound. We have walked the route of justice technology change, and we mark it for the courts and agencies that follow.
